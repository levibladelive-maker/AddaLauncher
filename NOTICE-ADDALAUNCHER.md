## What changed
- New package `com.movtery.zalithlauncher.addalauncher` (Studio, animated background, home screen, launch overlay).
- `MainScreen.kt`: the `LauncherMain` entry now shows `AddaHomeScreen`.
- `gradle.properties`: app name changed to AddaLauncher.
- The Minecraft launch engine, runtimes, renderers, accounts and downloaders are unchanged.
